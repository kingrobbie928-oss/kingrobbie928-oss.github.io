1 · The brief

One product, three kinds of people

I joined in my first year of college. My job was to sketch out rough drafts and layouts for screens aimed at three sets of users i.e. recruiters, managers and job seekers.

Who the screens are for

  1. RecruitersThey run the hiring pipeline and follow up with candidates and clients
  2. ManagersTracking team performance and following up with the client.
  3. Job seekersBuild a profile and find the right job.
2 · Screens

Draft screens for each kind of user

Rough layouts for recruiters, managers, job seekers and employers. Screens have information and data graphs relevant to each of them.

How a recruiter might start the day

Draft layouts for how a recruiter might start the day. It starts with the hiring pipeline by role, team performance, and follow ups with a calendar and meetings.

Every open role with its status

Each job runs across Applied, Screening, Test, Interview and Hired, with candidate counts in each stage. Beside it are candidates sourced, interviews conducted and hires, with a jobs summary and an applicants chart below.

How the team is doing

A team performance chart that filters by person, offers against the joining ratio, and weekly productivity with jobs posted, candidates sourced, interviews and joinings.

Follow ups

The first version had updates, a to do list and client actions. The later draft keeps updates and to dos on top and also brings in a calendar and today’s meetings.

The manager’s view

Outlines for a manager view has offers against joinings, hours saved, top performers and meetings, plus page layouts for teams, schedule, clients and insights.

Team, top performers and meetings

My Team as a grid of people, the top three performers with ratings, meetings as cards, and a slot for good news with a Send Kudos button.

Offers and Joining

Offers against joinings as a bar chart with filters such as hours saved, a performance summary for each team member, the top performers and a joining predictor.

Who is on the team, and who they look after

A Teams table with designation, years, ratings and clients handled, and a Clients page that filters by who handles each one.

Monthly progress

Meetings laid out on a monthly calendar, so a manager can see the team’s week without opening each one.

The numbers behind hiring

Cards for offers rolled out, interviews scheduled, database added, candidates reached, credits and sourcing. Each one has a client filter.

Open jobs with filters

Jobs filtered by notice period, experience, salary range, education and who handles the client, with a work mode, skills and location on the side.

Bringing in candidate data

A rough outline of a flow for bringing candidate data in and keeping it fresh. The flow is to upload a file or connect an ATS, map the columns, review the data and then download.

01Upload or connect an ATS

02Map CSV columns to the database

03Skill and age distribution

04Review candidates one by one

05Refreshed data by upload, age and reach

06Download the refined data

HR & Talent Acquisition

Layouts for searching candidates, posting a job from a JD, browsing open jobs, and managing clients and partners.

Search by uploading a file

Filters, upload, map and review run as steps on the top. Upload a file and then map its columns to the database before searching.

Post a job from a JD

Upload a job description and choose the client and add the skills required from recommendations. A confirmation then offers to create another profile or go to open jobs.

Every open role with filters

Open jobs with filters for notice period, experience, salary range and education, sort by relevance, and side filters for work mode, skills and location.

Clients and partners

Client companies as cards with an Add new client button, and partners split into companies and individuals.

What a job seeker will see

Layouts for what candidates will see. A full profile with quality score, skills, experience and a video resume, plus a job feed and search results.

A profile with a quality score

Contact details and socials, skill level shown in sliders, a quality analysis and availability, market value and expertise, and tabs for experience, education, applications, projects and certifications.

A feed of jobs to browse

Jobs from companies in the middle, filters for job type and industry on the left, and categories and top companies on the right.

Search results with filters

Results are shown for a role and location. They are sorted by salary or date, with filters for job type, experience level and location, and a Create Job Alert button.

The employer’s home

Draft layouts for the employer dashboard, subscription plans and account settings.

Employer dashboard

A welcome banner with counts for job posts, applications, meetings and hirings with their trends, an application response chart and recent job posts with their status.

Plans and account settings

Two plans side by side, Solo for free and Premium yearly, and account settings for the profile, linked accounts, privacy and deleting the account.
